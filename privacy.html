Privacy & Safety

Your privacy matters

MindEase is designed as a private space for reflection, awareness and understanding personal wellbeing patterns.

🔒

Private by design

This prototype keeps your check-in information in your browser using local storage. It is intended for personal reflection and demonstration purposes.

📋

What we collect

MindEase uses only information needed for the prototype experience.

  • Account information provided during registration.
  • Mood selected during a check-in.
  • Reported stress level.
  • Optional personal reflection text.
💾

Where data is stored

For this prototype, information is stored locally in your browser using Local Storage.

  • No backend database is required.
  • Data stays associated with this browser.
  • Clearing browser storage may remove saved data.
🧠

No diagnosis

MindEase is not a medical diagnostic system.

  • Patterns are observations, not diagnoses.
  • Insights are based on your check-in history.
  • The system does not label mental health conditions.
🤝

Support & safety

If a check-in contains language suggesting that someone may need urgent support, MindEase provides supportive guidance rather than making a diagnosis.

  • Consider speaking with someone you trust.
  • Professional support can also be helpful.
  • If you are in immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency support.

Important to remember

MindEase is a student prototype created for awareness, reflection and pattern recognition. It should not replace professional medical or mental health care. If you are concerned about your wellbeing, consider talking to a trusted person or qualified professional.
